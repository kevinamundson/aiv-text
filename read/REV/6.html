Revelation 6 DRAFT

The First Seal: The White Horse

1And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the sevena seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying as a voice of thunder, “Come!b”

2And I saw, and behold,c a white horse, and the one sitting on it having a bow; and a crown was given to him, and he went out conquering, and that he might conquer.

The Second Seal: War

3And when He opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, “Come!d”

4And another horse went out, fiery red; and to the one sitting on it, to him was given to take the peace from the earth, and that they will slay one another; and a great sword was given to him.

The Third Seal: Famine

5And when He opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, “Come!e” And I saw, and behold, a black horse, and the one sitting on it having a pair of scales in his hand.

6And I heard something likef a voice in the midst of the four living creatures, saying, “A choenix of wheat for a denarius,g and three choenixes of barley for a denarius; and you should not harm the oil and the wine.”

The Fourth Seal: Death

7And when He opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourthh living creature saying, “Come!”

8And I looked, and behold, a pale green horse, and the one sitting on it, the name of him was Death, and Hades was following with him; and authority was given to themi over the fourth of the earth, to kill with sword, and with famine, and with plague, and by the beasts of the earth.

The Fifth Seal: The Martyrs

9And when He opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those having been slain because of the word of God and because of the testimonyj that they had.

10And they cried out with a great voice, saying, “Until when, O Lord, holy and true, do You not judge and avenge our blood from those dwelling upon the earth?”

11And to each of them was given a white robe, and it was said to them that they will rest yet a little time, until their fellow servants would also be fulfilled, and their brothers, those being about to be killed even as they.

The Sixth Seal: Terror

12And I saw when He opened the sixth seal, and there was a great earthquake, and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the wholek moon became as blood,

13and the stars of heaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree casts its unripe figs being shaken by a great wind.

14And heaven departed as a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.

15And the kings of the earth, and the great ones, and the commanders, and the rich,l and the strong, and every slave andm free, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains.

16And they say to the mountains and to the rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the face of the One sitting on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb, n

17because the great day of Theiro wrath has come, and who is able to stand?”

Notes

  1. a 6.1 TR does not include seven.
  2. b 6.1 BYZ and TR Come and see!; also in verse 7.
  3. c 6.2 BYZ does not include and behold; similarly in verse 8
  4. d 6.3 TR Come and see!.
  5. e 6.5 RP and TR “Come and see!” Then I looked
  6. f 6.6 BYZ and TR do not include (something) like.
  7. g 6.6 Or A quart of wheat for a denarius. A choenix was a Greek dry measure equivalent to 1.92 pints or 0.91 liters. A denarius was customarily a day’s wage for a laborer; see Matthew 20:2.
  8. h 6.7 RP I heard the fourth
  9. i 6.8 RP to him
  10. j 6.9 BYZ includes of the Lamb.
  11. k 6.12 ALT, F35, and TR do not include whole.
  12. l 6.15 TR and the rich, and the commanders
  13. m 6.15 ALT, F35, and TR and every
  14. n 6.16 See Hosea 10:8.
  15. o 6.17 BYZ and TR His
