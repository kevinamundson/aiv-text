Acts 14 DRAFT

Paul and Barnabas at Iconium

1Now it came to pass in Iconium, according to the same, that they entered into the synagogue of the Jews and spoke so that a large multitude of both Jews and Greeks believed.

2But the unbelieving Jews stirred up and poisoned the souls of the Gentiles against the brothers.

3Therefore indeed they stayed a long time, speaking boldly for the Lord, the One testifying to the word of His grace, granting signs and wonders to be done through their hands.

4But the multitude of the city was divided, and indeed some were with the Jews, but some with the apostles.

5And as there was a rush of both the Gentiles and Jews, with their rulers, to mistreat and to stone them,

6having become aware, they fled to the Lycaonian cities Lystra and Derbe, and the surrounding region,

7and there they were preaching the gospel.

The Visit to Lystra and Derbe

8And a certain man in Lystra, powerless in the feet, was sitting, lame from the womb of his mother—who had never walked.

9This man heard Paul speaking, who, having looked intently at him and having seen that he has faith to be saved,

10said in a great voice, “Stand upright on your feet!” And he sprang up, and he was walking.

11And the crowds, having seen what Paul had done, lifted up their voice in Lycaonian, saying, “The gods, having become like men, have come down to us!”

12And Barnabas they were calling Zeus; and Paul, Hermes, because he was the leading speaker.

13And the priest of Zeus, being before the city,a having brought oxen and wreaths to the gates, with the crowds was desiring to sacrifice.

14But having heard, the apostles Barnabas and Paul, having torn their garments, rushed out into the crowd, crying out

15and saying, “Men, why do you⁺ do these things? We also are men of like nature with you⁺, proclaiming the gospel to you⁺ to turn from these vain things to the living God, who made the heaven and the earth and the sea and all the things in them,

16who in the generations past allowed all the nations to go their ways.

17And yet He did not leave Himself without witness, doing good, giving to you⁺b rains from heaven and fruitful seasons, filling your⁺c hearts with food and gladness.”

18And saying these things, they hardly stopped the crowds from sacrificing to them.

19But Jews came from Antioch and Iconium, and having persuaded the crowds, and having stoned Paul, they were dragging him outside the city, supposing him to have died.

20But the disciples having surrounded him, having risen up, he entered into the city. And on the next day he went out with Barnabas to Derbe.

Strengthening the Disciples

21And having proclaimed the gospel to that city and having discipled many, they returned to Lystra and to Iconium and to Antioch,

22strengthening the souls of the disciples, exhorting them to remain in the faith, and that, “through many tribulations it is necessary for us to enter into the kingdom of God.”

23And having appointed elders for them in every church, having prayed, with fasting they committed them to the Lord, in whom they had believed.

24And having passed through Pisidia, they came to Pamphylia,

25and having spoken the word in Perga, they went down to Attalia.

26And from there they sailed to Antioch, from where they had been delivered to the grace of God for the work that they had fulfilled.

27And having arrived and having gathered together the church, they were reporting all that God had done with them, and that He had opened a door of faith to the Gentiles.

28Andd they were staying no little time with the disciples.
