Acts 4 DRAFT

Peter and John before the Sanhedrin

1And in their speaking to the people, there came to them the priestsa and the captain of the temple and the Sadducees,

2being distressed because of their teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection out from the dead.

3And they laid hands on them and put them in custody until the next day, for it was already evening.

4But many of those having heard the word believed, and the number of the men became about five thousand.

5And it came to pass on the next day that their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem,

6and Annas the high priest, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and as many as were of high-priestly kindred.

7And having set them in the midst, they were inquiring, “In what power or in what name did you⁺ do this?”

8Then Peter, having been filled with the Holy Spirit, said to them, “Rulers of the people and elders,b

9if we this day are examined as to a good work to the ailing man, by whatc he has been healed,

10let it be known to all of you⁺, and to all the people of Israel, that in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth—whom you⁺ crucified, whom God raised out from the dead—in Him this man has stood before you⁺ sound.

11This is

‘the stone having been rejected by you⁺, the builders,

which has become the head of the corner.’ d

12And there is salvation in no other, for there is not another name under heavene having been given among men in which it is necessary for us to be saved.”

The Name Forbidden

13Now seeing the boldness of Peter and of John, and having comprehended that they are unlettered and ordinary men, they were marveling, and they were recognizing them, that they had been with Jesus.

14And seeing the man having been healed standing with them, they had nothing to contradict.

15But having commanded them to go away outside the Sanhedrin,f they were conferring with one another,

16saying, “What shall we do to these men? For indeed that a notable sign has come to pass through them is apparent to all those dwelling in Jerusalem, and we are not able to deny it.

17But so that it might not spread further on toward the people, let us threateng them to speak no longer in this name to any man.”

18And having summoned them, they charged them not to speak at all, nor to teach, in the name of Jesus.

19But Peter and John, answering, said to them, “Whether it is righteous before God to listen to you⁺ rather than God, you⁺ judge.

20For we are unable not to speak of what we saw and heard.”

21And having further threatened them, they let them go, finding no way how they might punish them, because of the people, because all were glorifying God for that having happened.

22For the man on whom this sign of healing had taken place was more than forty years old.

The Believers’ Prayer

(Psalm 2:1–12)

23Now having been let go, they went to their own and reported how much the chief priests and the elders had said to them.

24And having heard, with one accord they lifted up their voice to God and said, “Sovereign Lord, You madeh the heaven and the earth and the sea and all things that are in them,

25having said by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of Your servant,i our father David:j

‘Why did the nations rage

and the peoples devise vain things? k

26The kings of the earth took a stand,

and the rulers were gathered together the same place

against the Lord

and against His Christ.’l

27For in truth, they were gathered together in this citym against Your holy servant Jesus, whom You anointed—both Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel—

28to do whatever Your hand and Your purpose predestined to come to pass.

29And now, Lord, look upon their threats, and give to Your servants to speak Your word with all boldness,

30in You stretching out Your hand for healing and signs and wonders to come to pass through the name of Your holy servant Jesus.”

31And on their having prayed, the place in which they were assembled was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and they were speaking the word of God with boldness.

Sharing among Believers

(Acts 2:42–47)

32Now of the multitude of those having believed the heart and soul were one, and no one was saying anything of that being hisn to be his own, but all things were to them in common.

33And with great power the apostles were giving the testimony of the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was upon them all.

34For there was not even anyone in need among them, for as many as were owners of lands or houses, selling them, were bringing the proceeds of the things being sold

35and were laying them at the feet of the apostles, and distribution was being made to each, just as anyone had need.

36Now Josepho having been called Barnabas by the apostles (which is, being translated, Son of Encouragement), a Levite, a Cypriot by birth,

37having sold a field being his, brought the money and laid it at the feet of the apostles.
