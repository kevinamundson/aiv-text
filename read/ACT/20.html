Acts 20 DRAFT

Paul in Macedonia and Greece

1Now after the uproar had ceased, Paul, having summoned the disciples and having encouraged them,a having said farewell, went out to go to Macedonia.

2And having passed through those parts and having exhorted them with much talk, he came to Greece.

3And having continued three months, a plot having been made against him by the Jews, he being about to sail into Syria, there came a purpose to return through Macedonia.

4And he was accompaniedb by Sopater son of Pyrrhus, a Berean,c and Aristarchus and Secundus of the Thessalonians, and Gaius of Derbe, and Timothy, and the Asians Tychicus and Trophimus.

5But these, having gone ahead, were remaining for us in Troas.

6And we sailed away from Philippi after the days of the Unleavened,d and we came to them at Troas within five days, where we stayed seven days.

Eutychus Revived at Troas

(2 Kings 4:18–37)

7And on the first of the week, we having gathered togethere to break bread, Paul, was talking to them, about to depart on the next day, and was he continuing the talk until midnight.

8Now there were many lamps in the upper room where we weref gathered together.

9And a certain young man named Eutychus was sitting upon the window, borne down by deep sleep, Paul talking on longer. Having been borne down by sleep, he fell down from the third story and was lifted up dead.

10But Paul, having descended, fell upon him, and having embraced him, he said, “Do not be alarmed, for his life is in him!”

11And having gone up, and having broken the bread, and having tasted, and having talked for a considerable time until daybreak, so he went out.

12Then they brought the boy alive and were not just moderately comforted.

From Troas to Miletus

13But we, having gone ahead tog the ship, sailed to Assos, from there being about to take in Paul. For having arranged thus, he was intending himself to go on foot.

14And when he met with us at Assos, having taken him up, we came to Mitylene.

15And having sailed away from there, the next day we arrived opposite Chios, and the second day we arrived at Samos, andh the following day we came to Miletus.

16For Paul had decided to sail by Ephesus, so that it might not come upon him to spend time in Asia; for he was hastening, if it was possible for him, to be in Jerusalem on the day of Pentecost.i

Paul’s Farewell to the Ephesians

17And from Miletus, having sent to Ephesus, he called for the elders of the church.

18And when they came to him, he said to them, “You⁺ know from the first day on which I came to Asia, how I was with you⁺ the whole time,

19serving the Lord with all humility and tears,j and in trials having befallen me in the plots of the Jews;

20how I kept back nothing of that being profitable, to declare it to you⁺ and to teach you⁺ publicly and from house to house,

21earnestly testifying both to the Jews and to the Greeks repentance to God and faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.k

22And now behold, bound in the Spirit I go to Jerusalem, not knowing what will happen to me in it,

23except that the Holy Spirit fully testifies to mel in every city, saying that chains and tribulations await me.

24But on no account do I make my life dear to myself, so as to finish my coursem and the ministry that I received from the Lord Jesus: to testify fully to the gospel of the grace of God.

25And now, behold, I know that you⁺ will see my face no more—all of you⁺ among whom I went about proclaiming the kingdom. n

26Therefore I testify to you⁺ in this day that I am innocent of the blood of all.o

27For I did not shrink back from proclaiming to you⁺ the whole counsel of God.

28Take heedp to yourselves and to all the flock among whom the Holy Spirit has set you⁺ as overseers, to shepherd the church of God,q which He purchased with His own blood.

29I have known thatr after my departure, grievous wolves will come in among you⁺, not sparing the flock,

30and out from your⁺ own selves men will rise up, speaking perverse things to draw away disciples after them.

31Therefore stay awake, remembering that three years, night and day, I did not cease admonishing each one with tears.

32And now I commit you⁺s to God and to the word of His grace, being able to build you up and to give you an inheritance among all those having been sanctified.

33I coveted the silver or gold or clothing of no one.

34You yourselves know that these hands ministered to my needs and to those being with me.

35In everything I showed you⁺ that by thus laboring, it is necessary to aid those being weak, and also to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how He Himself said, ‘It is more blessed to give than to receive.’”

36And having said these things, having bowed his knee, he prayed with them all.

37Then there was much weeping among all, and having fallen upon the neck of Paul, they were kissing him,

38sorrowing especially over the word that he had spoken, that they are about to see his face no more. And they were accompanying him to the ship.
