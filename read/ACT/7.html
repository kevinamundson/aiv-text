Acts 7 DRAFT

Stephen’s Address: The Call of Abraham

(Genesis 12:1–9)

1And the high priest said, “Are these things so?”

2And he was saying: “Men, brothers and fathers, listen! The God of glory appeared to our father Abraham, being in Mesopotamia before his dwelling in Haran,

3and He said to him, ‘Go out of your country and from your kindred, and come into the land that I will show to you.’ a

4Then, having gone out from the land of the Chaldeans, he dwelt in Haran. And from there, after his father died, He transferred him into this land in which you⁺ now dwell.

5And He did not give to him an inheritance in it, not even the length of a foot; and He promised to give it to him for a possession, and to his seed after him—there being to him no child.

6And God spoke thus, that his seed will be a sojourner in a strange land, and they will enslave it and will mistreat it four hundred years.

7‘And the nation to which they will be enslaved, I will judge,’ God said, ‘and after these things they will come out and will serve Me in this place.’ b

8And He gave to him the covenant of circumcision; and thus he begat Isaac and circumcised him on the eighth day, and Isaac Jacob, and Jacob the twelve patriarchs.

Joseph Sold into Egypt

(Genesis 37:12–30)

9And the patriarchs, having envied Joseph, sold him into Egypt. And God was with him,

10and He rescued him out of all his tribulations, and He gave him favor and wisdom before Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he appointed him ruler over Egypt and over all his house.

11And there came a famine upon allc Egypt and Canaan, and great tribulation, and our fathers were not finding sustenance.

12Now Jacob, having heard there is grain in Egypt, sent forth our fathers first.

13And on the second time, Joseph was made known tod his brothers, and the family of Joseph became known to Pharaoh.

14And having sent, Joseph called for his father Jacob and all the kindred, seventy-five souls in all.

Israel Oppressed in Egypt

(Exodus 1:8–22)

15And Jacob went down into Egypt, and he died—he and our fathers,

16and they were carried overe into Shechem and were placed in the tomb that Abraham bought for a price of silver from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.f

17Now as the time of the promise that God professed to Abraham was drawing near, the people increased and multiplied in Egypt,

18until there arose another king over Egypt,g who had not known Joseph.

19This one, having dealt treacherously with our kindred, mistreated our fathers, making them expose their infants, that they would not live.

The Birth and Adoption of Moses

(Exodus 2:1–10; Hebrews 11:23–29)

20In that time Moses was born (and he was lovely to God),h who was brought up three months in the house of his father.

21And on his having been exposed, the daughter of Pharaoh took him up and brought him up for herself for a son.

22And Moses was educated in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, and he was powerful in his words and deeds.

The Rejection and Flight of Moses

(Exodus 2:11–22)

23Now when a period of forty years was fulfilled to him, it came upon his heart to visit his brothers, the sons of Israel.

24And having seen a certain one being wronged, he defended and did vengeance for the one being oppressed, having struck down the Egyptian.

25And he was supposing his brothers to understand that God through his hand is giving them salvation, but they did not understand.

26And on the following day, he appeared to those who were fighting, and he was urging them to peace, having said, ‘Men, you⁺ are brothers. Why are you⁺ wronging one another?’

27But the one wronging the neighbor pushed him away, having said, ‘Who appointed you ruler and judge over us?

28Do you want to kill me that way you killed the Egyptian yesterday?’ i

29Now Moses fled at this word, and he became a sojourner in the land of Midian, where he begat two sons.

The Call of Moses

(Exodus 3:1–22)

30And forty years having been fulfilled, an angelj appeared to him in the wilderness of Mount Sinai, in a flame of fire of a bush.

31And Moses, having seen, was marveling at the sight. And on his coming near to behold, there came the voice of the Lord:k

32‘I am the God of your fathers, the God of Abraham and of Isaac and of Jacob.’ And having become terrified, Moses was not daring to observe. l

33And the Lord said to him, ‘Loose the sandal of your feet, for the place on which you stand is holy ground.

34Having seen, I saw the oppression of My people in Egypt, and I heard their groans, and I came down to deliver them. And now come, I will send you to Egypt.’ m

35This Moses, whom they rejected, having said, ‘Who appointed you ruler and judge?’—this one whom God sent as both ruler and redeemer with the hand of the angel having appeared to him in the bush— n

36this one led them out, having done wonders and signs in the land of Egypt, and in the Red Sea, and in the wildernesso forty years.

37This is the Moses having said to the sons of Israel, ‘God will raise upp for you⁺ a prophet like me out from your⁺ brothers.’q

38This is the one having been in the congregation in the wilderness with the angel speaking to him in Mount Sinai, and with our fathers, who received living oraclesr to give to us,s

39to whom our fathers were not willing to be obedient, but pushed away, and they turned back in their hearts to Egypt, t

40having said to Aaron, ‘Make us gods who will go before us. For this Moses, who led us out from the land of Egypt, we do not know what happened to him.’ u

The Rebellion of Israel

(Exodus 32:1–35; Deuteronomy 9:7–29; Amos 5:16–27)

41And they made a calf in those days and brought up a sacrifice to the idol, and they were rejoicing in the works of their hands.

42But God turned away and delivered them up to serve the host of heaven, just as it has been written in the book of the prophets:

‘Did you⁺ offer slain beasts and sacrifices to Me

forty years in the wilderness, O house of Israel?

43And you⁺ took up the tabernacle of Molech

and the star of your⁺ god Rephan,

the figures that you⁺ made to worship them;

and I will remove you⁺

beyond Babylon.’v w

The Tabernacle of the Testimony

(Exodus 40:1–33; Hebrews 9:1–10)

44The tabernacle of the Testimony was with our fathers in the wilderness, just as the One speaking to Moses directed him to make it according to the figure that he had seen,

45and which, having received it by succession, our fathers brought in with Joshua in the possession of the nations whom God drove out from the face of our fathers, until the days of David,

46who found favor before God and asked to find a dwelling place for the God of Jacob. x

47But Solomon built for Him a house.

48But the Most High does not dwell in hand-made houses.y As the prophet says:

49‘Heaven is unto Me a throne,

and the earth a footstool for My feet.

What kind of house will you⁺ build for Me, says the Lord,

or what is the place of My rest?

50Did not My hand make all these things?’ z

51You⁺ stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heartsaa and ears! You⁺ always resist the Holy Spirit! As did your⁺ fathers, also you⁺ do.

52Which of the prophets did your⁺ fathers not persecute? And they killed those having foretold about the coming of the Righteous One, of whom you⁺ have now become betrayers and murderers,

53you who received the law by the ordination of angels, and did not keep it.”

The Stoning of Stephen

54Now hearing these things, they were cut in their hearts,ab and they were gnashing the teeth at him.

55But being full of the Holy Spirit, having looked intently into heaven, he saw the glory of God and Jesus standing at the right hand of God,

56and he said, “Behold, I see the heavens having been opened and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God.”

57And having cried out with a great voice, they held their ears and rushed with one accord upon him,

58and having cast him outside of the city, they were stoning him. And the witnesses laid aside their garments at the feet of a young man named Saul.

59And as they were stoning Stephen, he was calling out and saying, “Lord Jesus, receive my spirit.”

60And having laid down the knees, he cried out with a great voice, “Lord, do not place this sin on them.” And having said this, he fell asleep.
