Genesis 16 DRAFT

Hagar and Ishmael

1And Sarai, wife of Abram, had not borne to him; and to her was a maidservant, an Egyptianess, and her name was Hagar. 2And Sarai said to Abram, “Behold now, YHWH has restrained me from bearing. Go in, please, to my maidservant; perhaps I shall be built up from her.” And Abram listened to the voice of Sarai.

3And Sarai, wife of Abram, took Hagar the Egyptianess, her maidservant, at the end of ten years of Abram’s dwelling in the land of Canaan, and she gave her to Abram her husband, to him for a wife. 4And he went in to Hagar, and she conceived; and she saw that she had conceived, and her mistress became light in her eyes.a

5And Sarai said to Abram, “My violence is upon you! I, I gave my maidservant into your bosom, and she saw that she had conceived, and I became light in her eyes. May YHWH judge between me and between you.”

6And Abram said to Sarai, “Behold, your maidservant is in your hand. Do to her the good in your eyes.” And Sarai afflicted her, and she fled from her face.

7And the angelb of YHWH found her by the spring of waters in the wilderness, by the spring on the way to Shur. 8And he said, “Hagar, maidservant of Sarai, from where is this that you have come, and where are you going?”

And she said, “From the face of Sarai my mistress I am fleeing.”

9And the angel of YHWH said to her, “Return to your mistress and afflict yourself under her hands.” 10And the angel of YHWH said to her, “I will surely multiply your seed, and it shall not be counted for multitude.”

11And the angel of YHWH said to her,

“Behold, you are pregnant, and you shall bear a son,

and you shall call his name Ishmael,c

for YHWH has listened to your affliction.

12And he shall be a wild donkey of a man;

his hand against all,

and the hand of all against him,

and upon the face of all his brothers

he shall dwell.”

13And she called the name of YHWH, the One speaking to her, “You are El-Roi,d” for she said, “Even here I have seen after the One seeing me?” 14Upon thus the well was called Beer-lahai-roi.e Behold, it is between Kadesh and between Bered.

15And Hagar bore to Abram a son, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar bore, Ishmael. 16And Abram was a son of eighty years and six years when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.

Notes

  1. a 16.4 Or she began to despise her mistress
  2. b 16.7 Or Angel; also in verses 9, 10, and 11; corresponding pronouns may also be capitalized.
  3. c 16.11 Ishmael means God hears.
  4. d 16.13 El-Roi means the God who sees me
  5. e 16.14 Beer-lahai-roi means well of the Living One who sees me.
