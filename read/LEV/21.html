Leviticus 21 DRAFT

Holiness Required of Priests

1And YHWH said to Moses, “Speak to the priests, the sons of Aaron, and you shall say to them: No one shall make himself unlcean for a dead soul among his people,

2except for his close relative who are near to him—for his mother, and for his father, and for his son, and for his daughter, and for his brother,

3and for his sister, the virgin, who is near to him, who has not had a husband. He shall make himself unlean for her.

4He shall not make himself unclean as a husband among his people, to profane himself.

5They shall not make baldness on their heads, and they shall not shave the edges of their beards, and they shall not cut a cutting in their flesh.

6They shall be holy to their God, and they shall not profane the name of their God. For they bring near the fire offerings of YHWH, the bread of their God; and they shall be holy.

7And they shall not take a woman, a prostitute or defiled, and a woman cast out from her husband they shall not take, for he is holy to his God.

8And you shall sanctify him, for he brings near the bread of your God. He is holy to you, for holy am I, YHWH who sanctifies you⁺.

9And the daughter of a man, of a priest, if she profanes herself by playing the prostitute, she is profaning her father; she shall be burned with the fire.

10And the high priest among his brothers, on whose head is poured the anointing oil and who has filled his hand to put on the garments, shall not uncover his heada and he shall not tear his clothes.

11And he shall not go near any dead soul; he shall not make himself unclean for his father or for his mother.

12And he shall not go out of the sanctuary, and he shall not profane the sanctuary of his God; for the diadem of the anointing oil of his God is on him. I am YHWH.

13And he shall take a wife in her virginity.

14A widow, or a divorced woman, or a defiled woman—a prostitute—these he shall not take; but rather he shall take a virgin from his people as a wife.

15He shall not profane his seed among his people. For I am YHWH who sanctifies him.”

Restrictions against Those with Blemishes

16And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

17“Speak to Aaron, saying, ‘No man from your seed for their generations who has a blemish in him shall draw near to bring near the bread of his God.

18For any man who has a blemish in him shall not draw near—a man blind, or lame, or disfigured, or a limb too long,

19or a man who has in him a fracture of foot, or a fracture of hand,

20or a hunchback, or a dwarf, or a man who has a defect in his eye, or itch, or scab, or a crushed testicle.

21Any man from the seed of Aaron the priest who has a blemish in him shall not draw near to bring near the fire offerings to YHWH; he has a blemish in him. He shall not draw near to bring near the bread of his God.

22He may eat the bread of his God from the holies of holies,b and from the holy things.

23Only he shall not enter into the veil, and to the altar he shall not draw near, because a blemish is in him, and he shall not profane My sanctuaries, for I am YHWH who sanctifies them.’”

24And Moses spoke to Aaron, and to his sons, and to all the sons of Israel.

Notes

  1. a 21.10 Or shall not let his hair be unkempt
  2. b 21.22 Or most holy
