Leviticus 20 DRAFT

Punishments for Disobedience

(Leviticus 26:14–39; Deuteronomy 28:15–68)

1And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

2“And to the sons of Israel you shall say, ‘Any man from the sons of Israel and from the sojourner who is sojourning in Israel who gives from his seed to Molech, he must surely be put to death. The people of the land shall stone him with stones.

3And I will set My face against that man, and I will cut him off from among his people, for from his seed he has given to Molech, to defile My sanctuary and to profane My holy name.

4And if the people of the land ever hide their eyes from that man when he gives from his seed to Molech so as not to put him to death,

5then I will set My face against that man and against his family, and I will cut him off and all who prostitute themselves after him, to commit prostitution after Molech, from among their people.

6And the soul who turns to the mediums and to the spiritists, to prostitute himself after them, I will set My face against that soul, and I will cut him off from among his people.

7And you⁺ shall sanctify yourselves, and you⁺ shall be holy, for I am YHWH your⁺ God.

8And you⁺ shall keep My statutes and you⁺ shall do them. I am YHWH who sanctifies you⁺.

9For any man who curses his father and his mother, he must surely be put to death.a His father and his mother he has cursed; his blood is on him. b

Punishments for Sexual Immorality

(Proverbs 5:1–23; 1 Corinthians 5:1–8)

10And the man who commits adultery with the wife of a man—who commits adultery with the wife of his neighbor—the adulterer and the adulteress must surely be put to death.

11And the man who lies with the wife of his father has uncovered the nakedness of his father—both of them must surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

12And the man who lies with his daughter-in-law, both of them must surely be put to death. They have done a perversion; their blood is on them.

13And the man who lies with a male—the lyings of a woman—both of them have done an abomination. They must surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

14And the man who takes a woman and her mother, it is wickedness. They shall be burned in the fire, he and they, and there shall not be wickedness in your⁺ midst.

15And a man who gives his lying with an animal, he must surely be put to death, and the animal you⁺ shall kill.

16And the woman who draws near to any animal to lie with it, you shall kill the woman and the animal. They must surely be put to death; their blood is on them.

17And the man who takes his sister, a daughter of his father or a daughter of his mother, and he sees her nakedness, and she sees his nakedness,c it is a wicked thing, and they shall be cut off before the eyes of the sons of their people. He has uncovered the nakedness of his sister; he shall bear his guilt.

18And the man who lies with a menstruating woman, and he uncovers her nakedness,d he has exposed her flow, and she has uncovered the flow of her blood. And both of them shall be cut off from among their people.

19And the nakedness of the sister of your mother or the sister of your father you shall not uncover, because he has exposed his close relative. They shall bear their guilt.

20And the man who lies with his aunt, he has uncovered the nakedness of his uncle; they shall bear their sin. They shall die childless.

21And the man who takes the wife of his brother, it is impurity. He has uncovered the nakedness of his brother. They shall be childless.

Distinguish between Clean and Unclean

22And you⁺ shall keep all My statutes and all My judgments, and you⁺ shall do them, and the land where I am bringing you⁺ there to dwell in it will not vomit you⁺ out.

23And you⁺ shall not walk in the statutes of the nation that I am sending out from before your⁺ faces, for all these they did, and I abhor them.

24And I have said to you⁺ that you⁺ shall possess their land, and I will give it to you⁺ to possess it—a land flowing with milk and honey. I am YHWH your⁺ God, who has separated you⁺ from the peoples.

25And you⁺ shall separate between the clean and between the unclean animals, and between the unclean and between the clean birds; and you⁺ shall not make your⁺ souls detestable by the beast and by the bird and by all that crawls on the ground, which I have separated for you⁺ to be unclean.

26And you⁺ shall be holy to Me, for I, YHWH, am holy; and I have separated you⁺ from the peoples, to be to Me.

27And a man or a woman—when there is among them a medium or spiritist, they must surely be put to death. With stones they shall stone them. Their blood is on them.’”

Notes

  1. a 20.9 Or dishonors or reviles; similarly again in this verse
  2. b 20.9 Cited in Matthew 15:4 and Mark 7:10
  3. c 20.17 Or and they have sexual relations
  4. d 20.18 Or has sexual relations with her; similarly in verse 19
