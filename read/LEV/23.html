Leviticus 23 DRAFT

Feasts and Sabbaths

(Exodus 23:14–19)

1And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

2“Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, ‘The appointed times of YHWH that you⁺ shall proclaim to be holy convocations—these are My appointed times.

3Six days work shall be done, and the seventh day is a Sabbath of complete rest, a holy convocation. You⁺ shall not do any work; it is a Sabbath to YHWH in all your⁺ dwellings.

Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

(Exodus 12:14–28; Numbers 28:16–25; Deuteronomy 16:1–8)

4These are the appointed times of YHWH, holy convocations, which you⁺ shall proclaim them at their appointed time.

5On the first month, on the four and tenth of the month, between the evenings,a is the Passover to YHWH.

6And on the five and tenth day of this month is the Feast of the Unleavenedb to YHWH. Seven days you⁺ shall eat unleavened bread.

7On the first day you⁺ shall have a holy convocation. You⁺ shall not do any work of service.

8And you⁺ shall bring near a fire offering to YHWH for seven days. The seventh day is a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.’”

The Feast of Firstfruits

9And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

10“Speak to the sons of Israel, and you shall say to them, ‘When you⁺ come into the land that I am giving to you⁺, and you⁺ reap its harvest, then you⁺ shall bring in a sheaf of the firstfruits of your⁺ harvest to the priest.

11And he shall wave the sheaf before the face of YHWH to be accepted on your⁺ behalf. From the day after the Sabbath the priest shall wave it.

12And you⁺ shall do on that day of your⁺ waving the sheaf a male lamb, unblemished, a son of a year, for a burnt offering to YHWH.

13And its grain offering is two tenths of an ephah of fine flourc mixed with the oil, a fire offering to YHWH, a pleasing aroma. And its drink offering is of wine, a fourth of a hin.d

14And you⁺ shall not eat bread, and parched grain, and fresh grain, until this same day that you⁺ have brought an offering to your⁺ God; it shall be an everlasting statute throughout your⁺ generations in all your⁺ dwellings.

The Feast of Weeks

(Acts 2:1–13)

15And you⁺ shall count for yourselves from the day after the Sabbath, from the day of your⁺ bringing in the sheaf of the wave offering—they are seven completed Sabbaths—

16until the day after the seventh Sabbath. You⁺ shall count fifty days, and you⁺ shall bring near a new grain offering to YHWH.

17From your⁺ dwellings you⁺ shall bring bread of a wave offering, two loaves of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour; they shall be baked leavened, the firstfruits to YHWH.

18And you⁺ shall bring near with the bread seven lambs unblemished, sons of a year, and one bull, a son of the herd, and two rams; they shall be a burnt offering to YHWH, and their grain offerings and drink offerings, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to YHWH.

19And you⁺ shall do one kid of the goats for a sin offering, and two male lambs, sons of a year, for a sacrifice of peace offerings.

20And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits, a wave offering before the face of YHWH, with the two lambs; they shall be holy to YHWH for the priest.

21And you⁺ shall proclaim on this same day that it shall be a holy convocation to you⁺. You⁺ shall not do any work of service; it shall be an everlasting statute in all your⁺ dwellings throughout your⁺ generations.

22And in your⁺ reaping the harvest of your⁺ land, you shall not complete the edge of your field in your reaping, and you shall not gather the gleaning of your harvest; you shall leave them for the poor and for the sojourner. I am YHWH your⁺ God.’”

The Feast of Trumpets

(Numbers 29:1–6)

23And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

24“Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, ‘In the seventh month, on the first of the month, it shall be to you⁺ a complete rest, a memorial of trumpet blasting, a holy convocation.e

25You⁺ shall not do any work of service, and you⁺ shall bring near a fire offering to YHWH.’”

The Day of Atonement

(Leviticus 16:1–34; Numbers 29:7–11)

26And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

27“Indeed, on the tenth of this seventh month, it is the Day of Atonements. It shall be for you⁺ a holy convocation, and you⁺ shall afflict your⁺ soulsf and you⁺shall bring near a fire offering to YHWH.

28And you⁺ shall not do any work on this same day. For it is the Day of Atonements, to make atonement upon you⁺ before the face of YHWH your⁺ God.

29For any soul who is not afflicted on this very day, he shall even be cut off from his people.

30And any soul who does any work on this very day, I will even destroy that soul from among his people.

31You⁺ shall not do any work; it is an everlasting statute throughout your⁺ generations in all your⁺ dwellings.

32It is to you⁺ a Sabbath of complete rest, and you⁺ shall afflict your⁺ souls. On the ninth of the month at the evening, from evening to evening you⁺ shall celebrate your⁺ Sabbath.”

The Feast of Tabernacles

(Nehemiah 8:13–18; Zechariah 14:16–21)

33And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

34“Speak to the sons of Israel, saying, ‘On the five and tenth day of this seventh month is the Feast of Booths for seven days to YHWH. g

35On the first day is a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.

36Seven days you⁺ shall bring near a fire offering to YHWH. On the eighth day, for you⁺ there shall be a holy convocation, and you⁺shall bring near a fire offering to YHWH. It is a sacred assembly; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.

37These are the appointed times of YHWH which you⁺ shall proclaim them, holy convocations, to bring near a fire offering to YHWH, a burnt offering, and a grain offering, a sacrifice, and drink offerings—the matter of a day on its day—

38besides the Sabbaths of YHWH, and besides your⁺ gifts, and besides all your⁺ vows, and besides all your⁺ freewill offerings that you⁺ give to YHWH.

39Indeed on the five and tenth day of the seventh month, when you⁺ have gathered in the produce of the land, you⁺ shall keep the feast of YHWH for seven days. On the first day is a complete rest, and on the eighth day a complete rest.

40And you⁺ shall take for yourselves on the first day the fruit of majestic trees, branches of palms, and the boughs of leafy trees, and willows of the brook. And you⁺ shall rejoice before the face of YHWH your⁺ God for seven days. h

41And you⁺ shall keep it a feast to YHWH for seven days in the year, an everlasting statute in your⁺ generations. In the seventh month you⁺ shall celebrate it.

42You⁺ shall dwell in the boothsi for seven days; all the native in Israel shall dwell in the booths,

43so that your⁺ generations may know that I made the sons of Israel dwell in the booths when I brought them out from the land of Egypt. I am YHWH your⁺ God.’”

44And Moses declared the appointed times of YHWH to the sons of Israel.

Notes

  1. a 23.5 That is, at twilight
  2. b 23.6 That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.
  3. c 23.13 Two-tenths of an ephah is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour); also in verse 17.
  4. d 23.13 A fourth of a hin is approximately 0.97 quarts or 0.92 liters of wine.
  5. e 23.24 Or a sacred assembly
  6. f 23.27 Or humble yourselves or deny yourselves; also in verse 32
  7. g 23.34 That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as the Feast of Tabernacles or the Feast of Shelters and originally called the Feast of Ingathering (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).
  8. h 23.40 Or poplars
  9. i 23.42 Or tabernacles or shelters; twice in this verse, and also in verse 43; see the footnote for verse 34.
