Leviticus 22 DRAFT

Restrictions against the Unclean

1And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

2“Speak to Aaron and to his sons, and they shall be separated from the holy things of the sons of Israel, and they shall not profane My holy name that they are sanctifying to Me. I am YHWH.

3Say to them throughout your⁺ generations: Any man from all of your⁺ seed who draws near to the holy things that the sons of Israel are sanctifying to YHWH, and his uncleanness is upon him, that soul shall be cut off from before My face. I am YHWH.

4Any man from the seed of Aaron and who is a lepera or has a discharge—the holy things he shall not eat until that he is clean; and whoever touches anything unclean by a dead soul or a man from whom a lying of seed goes out,

5or a man who touches any swarming thing that is unclean to him or any person who is unclean to him—to all his uncleanness—

6the soul who touches it shall be unclean until the evening, and he shall not eat from the holy things, unless if he bathes his flesh in the water.

7And when the sun goes down, then he shall be clean, and afterward he may eat from the holy things, because it is his bread.

8A carcass and torn thing he shall not eat, to become unclean by it. I am YHWH.

9And they shall keep My charge, and they shall not bear sin for it, and die by it, because they profane it. I am YHWH who sanctifies them.

10And not any outsider shall eat a holy thing; a settler of a priest and a hireling shall not eat a holy thing.

11And if a priest buys a soul, the property of his silver, he may eat it. And one who is born of his house, they may eat his bread.

12And the daughter of a priest, if she becomes to an outsider, she may not eat of the contributions of the holy things.

13And the daughter of a priest, if she becomes a widow or a divorced woman and has no seed to her, and she has returned to the house of her father as in her youth, she may eat from the bread of her father, and not any outsider shall eat it.

14And if a man eats a holy thing in error,b then he shall add its fifth on it, and he shall give it to the priest the holy thing.

15And they shall not profane the holy things of the sons of Israel, which they raise up to YHWH,

16or cause them to bear the iniquity of guilt when they eat their holy things, for I am YHWH who sanctifies them.”

Worthy Offerings

17And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

18“Speak to Aaron and to his sons and to all the sons of Israel, and say to them, ‘Any man from the house of Israel and from the sojourners in Israel who brings near his sacrifice for any of his vows and for any of his freewill offerings that they bring near to YHWH for a burnt offering,

19for your⁺ acceptance, an unblemished male among the herd among the sheep or among the goats.

20Anything that has a blemish in it, you⁺ shall not bring near; for it shall not be accepted for you⁺.

21If a man he brings near a sacrifice of peace offerings to YHWH to fulfill a vow, or for a freewill offering among the herd or among the flock, it must be unblemished for acceptance; there shall not be any blemish in it.

22Blind, or broken, or maimed, or having a running sore, or itch, or scab—these you⁺ shall not bring near to YHWH, and a fire offering you⁺ shall not give from them on the altar to YHWH.

23And an oxc or a lamb with a limb too long or too short, you may do it as a freewill offering, but for a vow it shall not be accepted.

24And bruised, and crushed, and torn, and cut you⁺ shall not bring near to YHWH. And in your⁺ land you⁺ shall not do it.

25And from the hand of a son of a foreigner you⁺ shall not bring near the bread of your⁺ God from all of these, because their corruption is in them, a blemish in them; they shall not be accepted for you⁺.’”

26And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

27“When an oxd or a sheep or a goat is born, then it shall be seven days under its mother, and from the eighth day and thereafter it shall be accepted for an offering, a fire offering to YHWH.

28And an oxe or a sheep—do not slaughter both it and its son in one day.

29And when you⁺ sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to YHWH, for your⁺ acceptance you⁺ shall sacrifice it.

30On that day it shall be eaten; you⁺ shall not leave from it until morning. I am YHWH.

31And you⁺ shall keep My commandments, and you⁺ shall do them. I am YHWH.

32And you⁺ shall not profane My holy name, and I shall be shown holy in the midst of the sons of Israel. I am YHWH who sanctifies you⁺,

33the One bringing you⁺ out of the land of Egypt, to be your⁺ God. I am YHWH.”

Notes

  1. a 22.4 Forms of the Hebrew tzaraath, traditionally translated as leprosy, were used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.
  2. b 22.14 Or unintentionally
  3. c 22.23 Or a bull or a cow
  4. d 22.27 Or a calf
  5. e 22.28 Or a cow
