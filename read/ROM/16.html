Romans 16 DRAFT

Personal Greetings and Love

1Now I commend to you⁺ Phoebe our sister, being also a servanta of the church in Cenchrea, 2so that you⁺ might receive her in the Lord worthily of the saints, and that you⁺ might assist her in whatever matter she may need of you⁺. For she also has been a patroness of many, and of myself.

3Greet Priscab and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus— 4who laid down their own neck for my life, whom not only I thank, but also all the churches of the Gentiles— 5and the church at their house.

Greet Epenetus, my beloved, who is the firstfruit of Asiac for Christ.

6Greet Mary, who toiled much for you⁺.d

7Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners, who are notable among the apostles and who were in Christ before me.

8Greet Ampliatus,e my beloved in the Lord.

9Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in Christ, and my beloved Stachys.

10Greet Apelles, the approved in Christ.

Greet those of the household of Aristobulus.

11Greet Herodion, my kinsman.

Greet those of the household of Narcissus being in the Lord.

12Greet Tryphena and Tryphosa, those toiling in the Lord.

Greet Persis, the beloved, who toiled much in the Lord.

13Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord, and his mother and mine.

14Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas,f and the brothers with them.

15Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas and all the saints with them.

16Greet one another with a holy kiss.

All the churchesg of Christ greet you⁺.

Avoid Divisions

(Titus 3:9–11)

17Now I exhort you⁺, brothers, to watch out for those causing divisions and obstacles contrary to the teaching that you⁺ learned, and to turn away from them. 18For such do not serve our Lord Christ,h but their own belly; and through smooth talk and blessing they deceive the hearts of the innocent.

19For your⁺ obedience has reached to all. Therefore I rejoice over you⁺. But I want you⁺ to be wisei to good, and innocent to evil.

20And the God of peace will crush Satan under your⁺ feet in quickness. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christj be with you⁺.k

Greetings from Paul’s Fellow Workers

(Colossians 4:7–14)

21Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you⁺; so also do Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen.

22I, Tertius, the one having written the letter, greet you⁺ in the Lord.

23Gaius, the host of me and of the whole church, greets you⁺.

Erastus, the steward of the city, greets you⁺; so also does Quartus the brother.l

Doxology

(Jude 1:24–25)

25Now to Him being able to strengthen you⁺ according to my gospel and the proclamation of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery having been kept silent in the times of the ages, 26but now also having been made manifest through the prophetic writings, according to the commandment of the eternal God having been made known to all the Gentiles to the obedience of faithm— 27to the only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to Him be the glory unto the ages of the ages, Amen.n

  5. e 16.8 BYZ and TR Amplias
  6. f 16.14 BYZ and TR Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes
  7. g 16.16 BYZ and TR The churches
  8. h 16.18 BYZ and TR our Lord Jesus Christ
  9. i 16.19 BYZ and TR include indeed.
  10. j 16.20 NA, NE, and WH of our Lord Jesus
  11. k 16.20 Scrivener TR includes Amen.
  12. l 16.23 SBL, BYZ, and TR include 24The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you. Amen.
