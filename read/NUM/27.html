Chapter 27

The Daughters of Zelophehad

(Numbers 36:1–13)

1And the daughters of Zelophehad son of Hepher, son of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh, belonging to the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph, came near—and these are the names of his daughters: Mahlah, Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah.

2And they stood before the face Moses and before the face of Eleazar the priest and before the face of the leaders and all the congregation, at the door of the Tent of Meeting, saying,

3“Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the midst of the congregation, the ones assembling themselves against YHWH in the congregation of Korah; but he died in his own sin; and sons there were not to him.

4Why should the name of our father be removed from the midst of his clan, because there is not to him a son? Give to us a possession in the midst of the brothers of our father.”

5And Moses brought near their case before the face of YHWH,

6and YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

7“Rightly the daughters of Zelophehad are speaking. You surely must give to them a possession of inheritance in the midst of the brothers of their father, and you shall cause the inheritance of their father to pass over to them.

8And you shall speak to the sons of Israel, saying, ‘a man—If he dies and there is not to him a son, then you⁺ shall cause his inheritance to pass over to his daughter.

9And if there is not to him a daughter, then you⁺ shall give his inheritance to his brothers.

10And if there is not to him brothers, then you⁺ shall give his inheritance to the brothers of his father.

11And if there are no brothers to his father, then you⁺ shall give his inheritance to his close relative, the nearest to him, from his clan, and he shall possess it. And it shall be to the sons of Israel for a statute of judgment, as YHWH commanded Moses.’”

Moses Requests a Successor

(Deuteronomy 3:23–29)

12And YHWH said to Moses, “Go up into this mountain of the Abarim,a and see the land that I have given to the sons of Israel.

13And you shall see it, and you shall be gathered to your people, also you, as Aaron your brother was gathered,

14because you⁺ rebelled against My mouth in the Wilderness of Zin in the strife of the congregation, to sanctify Me at the waters before their eyes.” Those are the waters of Meribahb of Kadesh in the Wilderness of Zin.

15And Moses spoke to YHWH, saying,

16“Let YHWH, the God of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation

17who shall go out before them and who shall come in before them, and who will lead them out and who will bring them in, so that the congregation of YHWH may not be like sheep that there is not to them a shepherd.”

Joshua to Succeed Moses

(Deuteronomy 31:1–8)

18And YHWH said to Moses, “Take for yourself Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is the Spirit, and you shall lay your hand on him.

19And you shall cause him to stand before the face of Eleazar the priest and before the face of all the congregation, and you shall command him before their eyes.

20And you shall put some of your majesty on him, so that all the congregation of the sons of Israel may hear.

21And he shall stand before the face of Eleazar the priest, and he shall inquire for him by the judgment of the Urimc before the face of YHWH. At his mouth they shall go out, and at his mouth they shall come in—he and all the sons of Israel with him, and all the congregation.”

22And Moses did as YHWH had commanded him, and he took Joshua and caused him to stand before the face of Eleazar the priest and before the face of all the congregation,

23and he laid his hands on him, and he commanded him, as YHWH had spoken by the hand of Moses.

Notes

  1. a 27.12 LXX Go up Mount Nebo beyond the Jordan
  2. b 27.14 Meribah means quarreling; see Exodus 17:7.
  3. c 27.21 That is, the Lights
