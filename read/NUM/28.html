Numbers 28 DRAFT

The Daily Offerings

(Exodus 29:38–44)

1And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

2“Command the sons of Israel and say to them: My offering, My food for My fire offerings, an aroma pleasing to Me, you⁺ shall be careful to bring near to Me at their appointed time.

3And you shall say to them: This is the fire offering that you⁺ shall bring near to YHWH: Two male lambs, sons of a year, unblemished, day by day as a continual burnt offering.

4The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer in the evening,a

5and a tenth of an ephahb of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with pressed oil, a fourth of a hin.c

6It is a regular burnt offering, which was made at Mount Sinai for a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to YHWH.

7And its drink offering is a fourth of a hin for one lamb. In the Holy Place as a drink offering of strong drink to be poured out to YHWH.

8And the second lamb you shall offer in the evening. As the grain offering in the morning, and its drink offering, you shall offer as a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to YHWH.

The Sabbath Offerings

9And on the Sabbath day two lambs, sons of a year, unblemished, and two tenths of an ephahd of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with the oil, and its drink offering—

10this is the burnt offering for every Sabbath, besides the regular burnt offering and its drink offering.

The Monthly Offerings

11And at the beginnings of your⁺ months you⁺ shall bring near a burnt offering to YHWH: Two bulls, sons of the herd, and one ram and seven lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

12and three tenths of an ephahe of fine flour as a grain offering mixed with the oil for one bull, and two tenths of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering mixed with the oil for the one ram,

13and a tenth part of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering mixed with the oil for one lamb as a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to YHWH.

14And their drink offerings shall be half a hin of winef for a bull, a third of a hing for a ram, and a fourth of a hin for a lamb. This is the burnt offering for every month throughout the months of the year.

15And one kid of the goats as a sin offering to YHWH besides the regular burnt offering shall be offered, and its drink offering.

Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

(Exodus 12:14–28; Leviticus 23:4–8; Deuteronomy 16:1–8)

16And in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, is the Passover of YHWH.

17And on the fifteenth day of this month is the feast; for seven days unleavened bread shall be eaten.

18On the first day is a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.

19And you⁺ shall bring near a fire offering, a burnt offering to YHWH: Two bulls, sons of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs, sons of a year, unblemished; They shall be for you⁺.

20And their grain offerings are of fine flour mixed with the oil—you⁺ shall prepare three tenths of an ephah for a bull and two tenths for a ram.

21A tenth part of an ephah you shall prepare for the one lamb, for the seven lambs,

22and one goat as a sin offering, to make atonement upon you⁺.

23Besides the burnt offering of the morning, which is for a regular burnt offering, you⁺ shall prepare these.

24Like these, you⁺ shall prepare day by day, seven days, the food of the fire offering a pleasing aroma to YHWH; besides the regular burnt offering it shall be made, and its drink offering.

25And on the seventh day you⁺ shall have a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.

The Feast of Weeks

(Deuteronomy 16:9–12)

26And on the day of the firstfruits, when you⁺ bring near a new grain offering to YHWH, in your⁺ Weeks,h you⁺ shall have a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.

27And you⁺ shall bring near a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to YHWH, two bulls, sons of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs, sons of a year;

28and their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with the oil, three tenths of an ephah for the one bull, two tenths for the one ram,

29a tenth part of an ephah for the one lamb, for the seven lambs;

30one kid of the goats to make atonement upon you⁺.

31Besides the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, you⁺ shall present them and their drink offerings. They shall be unblemished.

Notes

  1. a 28.4 That is, at twilight; also in verse 8
  2. b 28.5 A tenth of an ephah is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour); also in verses 13, 21, and 29.
  3. c 28.5 Or a fourth of a hin of pure olive oil; that is, approximately 0.97 quarts or 0.92 liters; similarly in verses 7 and 14
  4. d 28.9 Two-tenths of an ephah is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour); also in verses 12, 20, and 28.
  5. e 28.12 Three-tenths of an ephah is approximately 6 dry quarts or 6.6 liters (probably about 7.6 pounds or 3.5 kilograms of flour); also in verses 20 and 28.
  6. f 28.14 Half a hin is approximately 1.9 quarts or 1.8 liters of wine.
  7. g 28.14 A third of a hin is approximately 1.3 quarts or 1.2 liters of wine.
  8. h 28.26 That is, during the Feast of Weeks, or Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).
