Numbers 29 DRAFT

The Feast of Trumpets

(Leviticus 23:23–25)

1“ And in the seventh month, on the first of the month, you⁺ shall have a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service. It is a day of sounding for you⁺.

2And you⁺ shall offer a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma to YHWH: One bull, a son of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

3and their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with the oil, three tenths of an ephaha for the bull, two tenthsb for the ram,

4and one tenthc for the one lamb, for the seven lambs,

5and one kid of the goats as a sin offering, to make atonement upon you⁺,

6besides the burnt offering with the New Moon and its grain offering, and the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings, according to their ordinance, a pleasing aroma, a fire offering to YHWH.

The Day of Atonement

(Leviticus 16:1–34; Leviticus 23:26–32)

7And on the tenth of this seventh month, you⁺ shall have a holy convocation, and you⁺ shall afflict your⁺ souls;d you⁺ shall not do any work.

8And you⁺ shall bring near a burnt offering to YHWH, a pleasing aroma: One bull, a son of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs, sons of a year; they are unblemished for you⁺,

9and their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with the oil, three tenths of an ephah for the bull, two tenths for the one ram,

10and a tenth part of an ephah for the one lamb, for the seven lambs,

11one kid of the goats as a sin offering, besides the sin offering the atonement, and the regular burnt offering and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

The Feast of Tabernacles

(Deuteronomy 16:13–17)

12And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month you⁺ shall have a holy convocation; you⁺ shall not do any work of service. And you⁺ shall keep a feast to YHWH seven days.

13And you⁺ shall bring near a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to YHWH, three and ten bulls, sons of the herd, two rams, and four and ten lambs, sons of a year; they shall be unblemished;

14and their grain offerings of fine flour mixed with the oil, three tenths of an ephah for the one bull, for the thirteen bulls, two tenths for the one ram, for the two rams,

15and a tenth part of an ephah for the one lamb, for the fourteen lambs,

16and one kid of the goats as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

17And on the second day: Twelve bulls, sons of the herd, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

18and their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

19and one kid of the goats as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, and its grain offering, and their drink offerings.

20And on the third day: Eleven bulls, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

21and their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

22and one goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, and its grain offering, and its drink offering.

23And on the fourth day: Ten bulls, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

24and their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

25and one kid of the goats as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

26And on the fifth day: Nine bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

27and their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

28and one goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, and its grain offering, and its drink offering.

29And on the sixth day: Eight bulls, two rams, and fourteen lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

30and their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

31and one goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offerings.

32And on the seventh day: Seven bulls, two rams, fourteen lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

33and their grain offerings and their drink offerings for the bulls, for the rams, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

34and one goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, its grain offering, and its drink offering.

35On the eighth day you⁺ shall have a sacred assembly; you⁺ shall not do any work of service.

36And you⁺ shall bring near a burnt offering, a fire offering, a pleasing aroma to YHWH: One bull, one ram, seven lambs, sons of a year, unblemished,

37and their grain offerings, and their drink offerings, for the bull, for the ram, and for the lambs, by their number, according to the ordinance,

38and one goat as a sin offering, besides the regular burnt offering, and its grain offering, and its drink offering.

39These you⁺ shall present to YHWH at your⁺ appointed times, in addition to your⁺ vows and your⁺ freewill offerings, for your⁺ burnt offerings, and for your⁺ grain offerings, and for your⁺ drink offerings, and for your⁺ peace offerings.”

40And Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that YHWH had commanded Moses.

Notes

  1. a 29.3 Three-tenths of an ephah is approximately 6 dry quarts or 6.6 liters (probably about 7.6 pounds or 3.5 kilograms of flour); also in verses 9 and 14.
  2. b 29.3 Two-tenths of an ephah is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour); also in verses 9 and 14.
  3. c 29.4 A tenth of an ephah is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour); also in verses 10 and 15.
  4. d 29.7 Or humble yourselves or deny yourselves
