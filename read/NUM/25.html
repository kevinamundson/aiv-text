Numbers 25 DRAFT

Moab Seduces Israel

(1 Corinthians 10:1–13)

1And Israel remained in Shittim,a and the people began to prostitute themselves with the daughters of Moab.

2And they called the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate, and they bowed down to their gods.

3And Israel joined himself to Baal of Peor, and the nose of YHWH burned against Israel.

4And YHWH said to Moses, “Take all the heads of the people, and hang them before YHWH before the sun, and the burning nose of YHWH will turn back from Israel.”

5And Moses said to the judges of Israel, “Each man must kill his men who joined themselves to Baal of Peor.”

The Zeal of Phinehas

6And behold, a man from the sons of Israel came, and he brought near to his brothers a Midianitess, before the eyes of Moses and before the eyes of all the congregation of the sons of Israel, and they were weeping at the door of the Tent of Meeting.

7And Phinehas son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the priest, saw, and he rose up from the midst of the congregation, and he took a spear in his hand,

8and he went after the man of Israel into the tent chamber, and thrust through the two of them, the man of Israel and the woman—through her belly. And the plague was restrained from upon the sons of Israel.

9And the ones dead in the plague were four and twenty thousand.

10And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

11“Phinehas son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, has turned back My wrath from the sons of Israel, in his being zealous with My zeal in their midst, and I did not consume the sons of Israel in My zeal.

12Therefore say, behold, I am giving to him My covenant of peace.

13And it shall be to him and to his seed after him a covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because that he was zealous for his God, and he made atonement upon the sons of Israel.”

14And the name of the man of Israel, the one struck, who was struck with the Midianitess, was Zimri son of Salu, a leader of a house of a father of the Simeonite.

15And the name of the woman, the one stuck, the Midianitess, was Cozbi the daughter of Zur; he was head of a tribe of a house of a father in Midian.

16And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

17“Harass the Midianites, and you shall strike them,

18for they are harassing you⁺ with their schemes by which they deceived you⁺ concerning the matter of Peor and concerning the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of a leader of Midian, their sister, the one struck in the day of the plague concerning the matter of Peor.”

Notes
