Numbers 8 DRAFT

The Lampstand

(Exodus 25:31–40; Exodus 37:17–24)

1And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

2“Speak to Aaron and you shall say to him, ‘When you lift up the lamps, the seven lamps shall give light toward the front of the face of the lampstand.’”

3And Aaron did so. He lifted up the lamps toward the front of the face of the lampstand, as YHWH had commanded Moses.

4And this was the workmanship of the lampstand: Hammered work of gold—up to its shaft, up to its flowers—it was hammered work; according to the appearance that YHWH had shown Moses, so he made the lampstand.

Cleansing the Levites

5And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

6“Take the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel and cleanse them.

7And thus you shall do to them to cleanse them: Sprinkle on them waters of purification, and they shall pass a razor on all their flesh, and they shall wash their clothes and make themselves clean.

8And they shall take a bull, a son of the herd, and its grain offering of fine flour mixed with the oil; and a second bull, a son of the herd, you shall take for a sin offering.

9And you shall bring near the Levites before the face of the Tent of Meeting, and you shall gather together all the congregation of the sons of Israel.

10And you shall bring near the Levites before the face of YHWH, and the sons of Israel shall lay their hands on the Levites.

11And Aaron shall wave the Levites as a wave offering before the face of YHWH from the sons of Israel, and they shall be to serve the service of YHWH.

12And the Levites shall lay their hands on the heads of the young bulls, and you shall do the one as a sin offering and the one as a burnt offering to YHWH, to make atonement upon the Levites.

13And you shall stand the Levites before the face of Aaron and before the face of his sons, and you shall wave them as a wave offering to YHWH.

14And you shall separate the Levites from the midst of the sons of Israel, and the Levites shall be Mine.

15And after that the Levites shall go in to serve the Tent of Meeting. And you shall cleanse them, and you shall wave them as a wave offering.

16For they are wholly given to Me from the midst of the sons of Israel. Instead of the opener of every womb—the firstborn of all the sons of Israel—I have taken them for Myself.

17For Mine are all the firstborn among the sons of Israel, among the man and among the beast. On the day of my striking all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I sanctified them to Myself.

18And I have taken the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the sons of Israel.

19And I have given the Levites—given ones—to Aaron and to his sons from the midst of the sons of Israel, to serve the service of the sons of Israel in the Tent of Meeting, and to make atonement upon the sons of Israel, and there will be no plague among the sons of Israel in the drawing near of the sons of Israel to the sanctuary.”

20And Moses and Aaron and all the congregation of the sons of Israel did to the Levites according to all that YHWH commanded Moses concerning the Levites; so the sons of Israel did to them.

21And the Levites cleansed themselves from sin, and they washed their clothes, and Aaron waved them as a wave offering before the face of YHWH, and Aaron made atonement upon them to cleanse them.

22And after that, the Levites went in to serve their service in the Tent of Meeting before the face of Aaron and before the face of his sons. As YHWH commanded Moses concerning the Levites, so they did to them.

Retirement for Levites

23And YHWH spoke to Moses, saying,

24“This is what is for the Levites: From a son of five and twenty years and above, he shall enter to perform the service in the work of the Tent of Meeting.

25And from a son of fifty years, he shall return from the host of the service, and he shall not serve anymore.

26And he shall minister with his brothers in the Tent of Meeting to keep the charge, and they shall not do work. Thus you shall do for the Levites in their charges.”
