Deuteronomy 34 DRAFT

The Death of Moses

1And Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, which is across from Jericho. And YHWH showed him all the land of Gilead, as far as Dan,

2and all Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah, as far as the Western Sea,a

3and the Negev, and the plain of the Valley of Jericho (the City of Palms), as far as Zoar.

4And YHWH said to him, “This is the land that I swore to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, saying, ‘To your seed I will give it.’ I have caused you to see it with your eyes, and there you shall not pass over.”

5And Moses the servant of YHWH died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of YHWH.

6And He buriedb him in a valley in the land of Moab, opposite Beth-peor; no man knows his grave to this day.

7And Moses was a son of a hundred and twenty years when he died; his eyes were not dim, nor had his vigor fled.

8And the sons of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days; and the days of weeping and mourning for Moses ended.

9And Joshua son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands on him. And the sons of Israel listened to him and did as YHWH had commanded Moses.

10And there has not arisen since that time a prophet in Israel like Moses, whom YHWH knew face to face,

11in all the signs and the wonders that YHWH sent him to do in the land of Egypt before Pharaoh, and before all his servants, and in all his land,

12and by all the mighty power and all the great terror,c which Moses performed in the eyes of all Israel.

Notes

  1. a 34.2 That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea
  2. b 34.6 SP and some LXX manuscripts they buried
  3. c 34.12 Or awesome deeds
