The Command to Leave Horeb

(Exodus 33:1–6)

1These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel beyond the Jordan in the wilderness, in the Arabah opposite Suph between Paran and between Tophel and Laban and Hazeroth and Dizahab.

2It is one and ten days from Horeba by way of Mount Seir to Kadesh-barnea.

3And it came to pass in the fortieth year, in the one and tenth month, on the first of the month, that Moses spoke to the sons of Israel according to all that YHWH had commanded him concerning them,

4after his striking of Sihon king of the Amorite, who dwelt in Heshbon, and Og king of Bashan, who dwelt at Ashtaroth, in Edrei.

5Beyond the Jordan, in the land of Moab, Moses began to make plain this law, saying:b

6YHWH our God spoke to us in Horeb, saying: “You⁺ have dwelt long enough at this mountain.

7Turn and set out for yourselves, and go to the hill country of the Amorite and to all its neighbors in the Arabah, in the hill country, and in the Shephelah, and in the Negev, and on the seacoast—the land of the Canaanite, and Lebanon, as far as the great river, the River Euphrates. c

8See, I have set before your⁺ faces the land. Go in and possess the land that YHWH swore to your⁺ fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give to them and to their seed after them.”

Moses Appoints Leaders

(Exodus 18:13–27)

9And I spoke to you⁺ at that time, saying, “I am not able by myself to bear you⁺.

10YHWH your⁺ God has multiplied you⁺, and behold, you⁺ are today as the stars of the heavens for multitude.

11May YHWH, God of your⁺ fathers, add to you⁺ as a thousand times, and bless you⁺ as He has spoken to you⁺.

12How can I bear by myself your⁺ trouble and your⁺ burden and your⁺ strife?

13Give for yourselves men wise and understanding and known to your⁺ tribes, and I will appoint them as your⁺ heads.”

14And you⁺ answered me and said, “Good is the thing that you have told us to do.”

15And I took the heads of your⁺ tribes, men wise and known, and I appointed them heads over you⁺—leaders of thousands, and leaders of hundreds, and leaders of fifties, and leaders of tens, and officers for your⁺ tribes.

16And I commanded your⁺ judges at that time, saying, “Listen between your⁺ brothers and you⁺ should judge righteously between a man and between his brother and between his sojourner.

17You⁺ shall not recognize faces in judgment; as the small, as the great, you⁺ shall hear. You⁺ shall not be afraid of the face of a man, for the judgment, it belongs to God. And the word that is too hard for you⁺, you⁺ shall bring near to me, and I will hear it.”

18And I commanded you⁺ at that time all the things that you⁺ should do.

Twelve Spies Sent Out

(Numbers 13:1–33)

19And we set out from Horeb and we went through all that great and fearful wilderness, which you⁺ saw, the way of the hill country of the Amorite, as YHWH our God had commanded us; and we came to Kadesh-barnea,

20and I said to you⁺, “You⁺ have come to the hill country of the Amorite, which YHWH our God is giving to us.

21See, YHWH your God has set before your face the land. Go up, possess it, as YHWH, God of your fathers, has spoken to you. Do not fear or be discouraged.”

22And all of you⁺ came near to me and said, “Let us send men before us, and let them search out for us the land, and bring back to us word—the way by which we should go up into it, and of the cities into which we shall come.”

23And the word was good in my eyes, and I took from among you⁺ two and ten men, one man for the tribe.

24And they turned, and they went up into the hill country, and they came to the Valley of Eshcol and spied it out.

25And they took in their hand from the fruit of the land, and they brought it down to us, and they brought back to us word and said, “Good is the land that YHWH our God is giving to us.”

Israel’s Rebellion

(Numbers 14:1–12)

26And you⁺ were not willing to go up, and you⁺ rebelled against the mouth of YHWH your⁺ God.

27And you⁺ murmured in your⁺ tents and said, “In YHWH’s hatred of us, He has brought us out of the land of Egypt to give us into the hand of the Amorite, to destroy us.

28Where are we going up? Our brothers have melted our hearts, saying, ‘A people greater and taller than we, cities great and fortified in the heavens, and also we saw the sons of the Anakim there.’”

29And I said to you⁺, “Do not be terrified and do not be afraid of them.

30YHWH your⁺ God, the One going before your⁺ faces—He will fight for you⁺, according to all that He did with you⁺ in Egypt before your⁺ eyes,

31and in the wilderness, where you saw how YHWH your God carried you, as a man carries his son, in all the way that you⁺ went, until your⁺ coming to this place.”

32And in this thing you⁺ are not believing in YHWH your⁺ God,

33the One going before your⁺ faces in the way, to seek out for you⁺ a place for your⁺ camping—in the fire by night, to show you⁺ the way in which you⁺ should go, and in the cloud by day.

Israel’s Penalty

(Numbers 14:20–35)

34And YHWH heard the sound of your⁺ words, and He was angry and swore, saying,

35“Surely not one of these men of this evil generation shall see the good land of which I swore to give to your⁺ fathers—

36except Caleb son of Jephunneh. He shall see it, and to him I am giving the land that he walked in, and to his sons, on account of that he has been fully after YHWH.”

37Also YHWH was angry with me for your⁺ sakes, saying, “Even you shall not go in there.

38Joshua son of Nun who stands before you, he shall go in there; strengthen him, for he shall cause Israel to inherit it.

39And your⁺ little ones, whom you⁺ said would be for plunder, and your⁺ sons, who have no knowledge today of good and evil, they shall go in there, and to them I will give it, and they shall possess it.

40And as for you⁺, turn and set out for yourselves into the wilderness by the way of the Red Sea.d”

The Defeat at Hormah

(Numbers 14:40–45)

41And you⁺ answered and said to me, “We have sinned against YHWH. We will go up and fight, according to all that YHWH our God commanded us.” And you⁺ had girded on, every man, of his weapons of war, and you⁺ were ready to go up into the mountain.

42And YHWH said to me, “Tell them: Do not go up, nor fight, for I am not among you⁺, lest you⁺ be stricken before the faces of your⁺ enemies.”

43And I spoke to you⁺ and you⁺ would not listen, and you⁺ rebelled against the mouth of YHWH and proudly went up into the mountain.

44And the Amorite who dwelt in that mountain came out against you⁺ and chased you⁺ like the bees do, and drove you⁺ back from Seir to Hormah.

45And you⁺ returned and you⁺ wept before the face of YHWH; YHWH would not listen to your⁺ voice, nor give ear to you⁺.

46And you⁺ remained in Kadesh many days, according to the days that you⁺ had dwelt.

Notes

  1. a 1.2 That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai; also in verses 6 and 19
  2. b 1.5 Note that Deuteronomy 1:6 through Deuteronomy 4:40 may be presented as a continuous section of unbroken speech by Moses. In place of multiple levels of nested quotes, this section has been set apart with a double space.
  3. c 1.7 Or foothills or lowlands; that is, the western foothills of Judea
  4. d 1.40 Or the Sea of Reeds
