Deuteronomy 16 DRAFT

Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

(Exodus 12:14–28; Leviticus 23:4–8; Numbers 28:16–25)

1Keep the month of Abib,a and you shall do the Passover to YHWH your God, for in the month of Abib YHWH your God brought you out of Egypt by night.

2And you shall sacrifice the Passover to YHWH your God, of the flock and of the herd, in the place where YHWH will choose to cause His Name to dwell there.

3You shall not eat leavened bread with it; seven days you shall eat with it unleavened bread, the bread of affliction—for in haste you came out of the land of Egypt—so that you may remember the day of your coming out of the land of Egypt all the days of your life.

4And there shall not be seen among you leaven in all your territory seven days, and nothing from the flesh that you sacrifice in the evening on the first day shall remain overnight until the morning.

5You are not able to sacrifice the Passover within one of your gates that YHWH your God is giving to you;

6but only at the place where YHWH your God will choose to cause His Name to dwell, there you shall sacrifice the Passover in the evening, at the going down of the sun, the appointed time of your coming out from Egypt.

7And you shall cook and you shall eat in the place that YHWH your God will choose, and you shall turn in the morning and go to your tents.

8Six days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a sacred assembly to YHWH your God; you shall do no work.

The Feast of Weeks

(Numbers 28:26–31)

9Seven weeks you shall count for yourself—from beginning the sickle among the grain you shall begin to count seven weeks.

10And you shall do the Feast of Weeksb to YHWH your God with the tribute of a freewill offering of your hand, which you shall give as YHWH your God blesses you.

11And you shall rejoice before the face of YHWH your God—you, and your son, and your daughter, and your manservant, and your maidservant, and the Levite who is within your gates, and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow who are among you, at the place where YHWH your God will choose to cause His Name to dwell there.

12And you shall remember that you were a slave in Egypt, and you shall keep and you shall do these statutes.

The Feast of Tabernacles

(Numbers 29:12–40)

13The Feast of Boothsc you shall do for yourself seven days, when you have gathered from your threshing floor and from your winepress.

14And you shall rejoice in your feast—you, and your son, and your daughter, and your manservant, and your maidservant, and the Levite, and the sojourner, and the fatherless, and the widow who are within your gates.

15Seven days you shall celebrate a feast to YHWH your God in the place that YHWH will choose, because YHWH your God will bless you in all your produce and in all the work of your hands, and you shall be only joyful.

16Three times in the year all your males shall appear before the face of YHWH your God in the place that He will choose: At the Feast of the Unleavened,d and at the Feast of Weeks,e and at the Feast of Booths.f And he shall not appear before the face of YHWH emptily—

17each man according to the gift of his hand, according to the blessing of YHWH your God that He has given to you.

Judges and Justice

18Judges and officers you shall give for yourself within all your gates that YHWH your God is giving to you, according to your tribes, and they shall judge the people with righteous judgment.

19You shall not bend judgement; you shall not recognize faces; and you shall not take a bribe, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and twists the words of the righteous.

20Righteousness, righteousness you shall pursue, so that you may live and possess the land that YHWH your God is giving to you.

Forbidden Forms of Worship

21You shall not plant for yourself an Asherah pole of any tree near the altar of YHWH your God that you shall make for yourself.

22And you shall not raise up for yourself a pillar, which YHWH your God hates.

Notes

  1. a 16.1 Abib was the first month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April; twice in this verse.
  2. b 16.10 That is, Shavuot; see footnotes for verse 16.
  3. c 16.13 Or Tabernacles or Shelters; see footnotes for verse 16.
  4. d 16.16 That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.
  5. e 16.16 That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Harvest (see Exodus 23:16) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).
  6. f 16.16 That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as the Feast of Tabernacles or the Feast of Shelters and originally called the Feast of Ingathering (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).
