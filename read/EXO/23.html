Exodus 23 DRAFT

Justice and Mercy

1“ You shall not lift up a vain report. Do not put your hand with the wicked to be a violent witness.

2You shall not follow a crowd to do evil, and you shall not testify in a dispute to turn aside after many to turn aside justice.

3And to a poor man you shall not honor in his dispute.

4If you encounter the ox of your enemy or his donkey going astray, you shall surely return it to him.

5If you see the donkey of one who hates you lying under its burden, then do not leave it; you must surely help him with it.

6You shall not turn aside the justice of your poor in his dispute.

7From a false matter you shall keep far, and the innocent and righteous you shall not kill, for I will not justify the wicked.

8And a bribe you shall not take, for the bribe blinds the clear-sighted and perverts the words of the righteous.

9And a sojourner you shall not oppress, for you⁺ yourselves know the soul of a sojourner, because you⁺ were sojourners in the land of Egypt.

Sabbath Laws

(Leviticus 25:1–7; Deuteronomy 15:1–6)

10And for six years you shall sow your land and gather its produce.

11And the seventh year you shall let it rest and let it lie fallow, and the poor of your people shall eat. And what they leave, the beast of the field shall eat. You shall do likewise for your vineyard and for your olive grove.

12Six days you shall do your works, and on the seventh day you shall rest, so that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your maidservant and the sojourner may be refreshed.

13And take heed in all that I have said to you⁺. And the name of other gods you⁺ shall not mention, nor let it be heard on your mouth.

The Three Feasts of Pilgrimage

(Leviticus 23:1–3)

14Three times in the year you shall keep a feast to Me.

15The Feast of the Unleaveneda you shall keep; seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you, at the appointed time in the month of Abib;b for in it you came out of Egypt. And none shall appear before My face empty.

16And the Feast of the Harvest, the firstfruits of your labors that you sow in the field;

and the Feast of the Ingatheringc at the end of the year, when you have gathered in your labors from the field. d

17Three times in the year all your males shall appear before the face of the Lord YHWH.

18You shall not offer the blood of My sacrifice on leavened bread, nor shall the fat of My sacrifice remain until morning.

19The first of the firstfruits of your ground you shall bring into the house of YHWH your God.

You shall not boil a young goat in the milk of its mother.

God’s Angel to Lead

(Deuteronomy 7:12–26)

20Behold, I am sending an angel before your face to guard you on the way and to bring you to the place that I have prepared.

21Be guarded from his face and listen to his voice; do not rebel against him, for he will not pardon your⁺ transgressions, for My name is within him.

22But if you will listen carefully to his voice and do all that I speak, then I will be an enemy to your enemies and an adversary to your adversaries.

23For My angel will go before your faces and bring you in to the Amorite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Canaanite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite; and I will cut them off.

24You shall not bow down to their gods, and you shall not serve them, and you shall not do according to their works; for you must utterly demolish them and thoroughly break to pieces their pillars.

25And you⁺ shall serve YHWH your⁺ God, and He will blesse your bread and your water, and I will take away sickness from among you.

26There will be no one miscarrying or barren in your land; the number of your days I will fulfill.

27My terror I will send before your faces, and I will confuse all the people among whom you come in; and I will give the neck of all your enemies to you.

28And I will send the hornet before your faces, and it shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite from before your faces.

29I will not drive them out from before your faces in one year, lest the land become desolate and the beast of the field become too numerous for you.

30Little by little I will drive them out from before your faces, until that you are fruitful and you take possession of the land.

31And I will set your border from the Red Seaf and to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the River.g For I will give into your⁺ hands those dwelling in the land, and you shall drive them out from before your faces.

32You shall not cut a covenant with them or with their gods.

33They shall not dwell in your land, lest they cause you to sin against Me. For if you serve their gods, it will surely be a snare to you.”

Notes

  1. a 23.15 That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.
  2. b 23.15 Abib was the first month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April.
  3. c 23.16 That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as the Feast of Weeks (see Exodus 34:22) or the Feast of Pentecost (see Acts 2:1).
  4. d 23.16 That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is later called the Feast of Tabernacles (or Booths or Shelters).
  5. e 23.25 LXX and Vulgate and I will bless
  6. f 23.31 Or the Sea of Reeds
  7. g 23.31 That is, the Euphrates
