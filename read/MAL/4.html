Malachi 4 DRAFT

The Day of Yahweh

(Zephaniah 1:7–18; 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11; 2 Peter 3:8–13)

1“For behold, the day is coming, burning like a furnace, and all the proud and all those doing wickedness will be as stubble; and the day that is coming will burn them up,” says YHWH of Hosts, “So that it will not leave for them a root or branch!”

2“And for you⁺ who fear My name the sun of righteousness will arise, with healing in its wings.a And you⁺ will go forth, and you⁺ will leap like calves from the stall.

3And you⁺ will trample the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your⁺ feet, on the day that I am preparing,” says YHWH of Hosts.

4“Remember the Law of Moses My servant that I commanded him on Horebb for all Israel—the statutes and ordinances.

5Behold, I am sending to you⁺ Elijah the prophet, before the face of the coming of the day of YHWH great and awesome. c

6And he will turn the heart of the fathers to the sons, and the heart of sons to their fathers,d lest I come and strike the land with a curse.”

Notes

  1. a 4.2 Or the Sun of Righteousness will rise with healing in His wings
  2. b 4.4 That is, Mount Sinai, or possibly a mountain in the range containing Mount Sinai
  3. c 4.5 Or dreadful; LXX glorious
  4. d 4.6 Cited in Luke 1:17
