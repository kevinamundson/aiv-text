Psalm 100 DRAFT

Make a Joyful Noise

(Psalm 66:1–20)

A Psalm of thanksgiving.

1 Shout to YHWH,

all the earth.

2Serve YHWH with gladness!

Come before His face with a shout of joy!

3Know that YHWH, He is God;

He has made us and not we ourselves;a

we are His people and the sheep of His pasture.

4Enter His gates with thanksgiving,

His courts with praise;

give thanks to Him; bless His name!

5For YHWH is good;

forever is His loving devotion,

and unto generation and generation His faithfulness.

Notes

  1. a 100.3 Or and we are His
