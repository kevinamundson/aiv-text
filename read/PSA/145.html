Psalm 145

I Will Exalt You, My God and King

A Psalm of David.†

1 I will lift You high, my God, the King,

and I will bless Your name forever and ever! a

2With every day, I will bless You,

and I will praise Your name, forever and ever!

3Great is YHWH and to be praised greatly,

and of His greatness there is no searching out.

4Generation to generation will commend Your works,

and Your mighty acts they will declare.

5The splendor of the glory of Your majesty,

and words of Your wonders I will put forth.

6And the strength of Your fearful acts they will speak,

and Your greatness I will recount.

7The memory of the abundance of Your goodness they will pour forth,

and Your righteousness they will sing out.

8Gracious and compassionate is YHWH,

slow to anger and great in loving devotion.

9Good is YHWH to all,

and His mercies are upon all His works.

10O YHWH, all Your works will give You thanks,

and Your holy ones will bless You.

11The glory of Your kingdom they will speak,

and Your might they will declare,

12to make known to the sons of man His mighty deeds,

and the glory of the majesty of His kingdom.

13Your kingdom is a kingdom of all ages,

and Your dominion is in every generation and generation.b

YHWH is faithful in all His words

and kind in all His actions.

14YHWH is upholding all those falling,

and raising up all those bent down low.

15The eyes of all wait for You,

and You give to them their food in its time.

16You open Your hand,

and satisfy the desire of every living thing.

17Righteous is YHWH in all His ways,

and kind in all His works.

18Near is YHWH to all those calling on Him,

to all who call on Him in truth.

19He does the desire of those who fear Him,

and their cry for help He hears,

and He saves them.

20YHWH is guarding all those loving Him,

and all the wicked He will destroy.

21My mouth will declare the praise of YHWH,

and all flesh blesses the name of His holiness,

forever and ever!

Notes

  1. a 145.1 This psalm is an acrostic poem, each verse beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.
  2. b 145.13 The final two lines are supplied by one MT manuscript, LXX, Syriac, and DSS.
