Psalm 134 DRAFT

Bless Yahweh, All You Servants

A Song of the Ascents.

1 Come, bless YHWH,

all servants of YHWH,

those standing in the house

of YHWH by night!

2Lift up your⁺ hands to the holy place,

and bless YHWH!

3May YHWH bless you from Zion,

Maker of heavens and earth!
