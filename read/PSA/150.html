Psalm 150

Let Everything That Has Breath Praise Yahweh

1Hallelu YAH!a

Praise God in His sanctuary;

praise Him in His mighty expanse!b

2Praise Him for His mighty acts;

praise Him according to the abundance of His greatness!

3Praise Him with the blast of the shofar;

praise Him with the harp and lyre!

4Praise Him with tambourine and dancing;

praise Him with strings and flute!

5Praise Him with cymbals sounding;

praise Him with cymbals shouting!

6Let everything that has breath praise YAH!

Praise YAH!

Notes

  1. a 150.1 Hallelu YAH means Praise YHWH; also in verse 6
  2. b 150.1 Or in His mighty heavens or in the firmament of His might; see also Genesis 1:6–8.
