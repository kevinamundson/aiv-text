Psalm 87 DRAFT

Yahweh Loves the Gates of Zion

Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm. A song.

1 His foundation

is on the holy mountains.a

2YHWH loves the gates of Zion

above all the dwellings of Jacob.

3Glorious things are ascribed to you,

O city of God.

Selah

4“I will mention Rahabb and Babylon

among those who know Me—

behold Philistia and Tyre, with Cush:c

This one was born there.

5And of Zion it will be said:

“This man and that man were born in her;

and He, the Most High, will establish her.”

6YHWH will record in the register of the peoples:

“This one was born there.”

Selah

7and those singing, also as players on instruments:

“All my springs are in you.”

A Song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah, for the one directing. According to Mahalath Leannoth.d

Notes

  1. a 87.1 Or He has founded His city on the holy mountains
  2. b 87.4 Rahab is a poetic name for Egypt.
  3. c 87.4 That is, the upper Nile region
  4. d 87.7 Mahalath Leannoth is probably a musical or liturgical term; see also Psalm 53:1.
