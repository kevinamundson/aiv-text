Psalm 50 DRAFT

The Mighty One Calls

A Psalm of Asaph.

1 God of gods, YHWH,

speaks and calls the earth,

from the rising of the sun to the going down.

2From Zion, the perfection of beauty,

God shines forth.

3Our God comes and will not be silent;

before His face a fire devours,

and all around Him it will storm exceedingly.

4He calls to the heavens from above,

and to the earth, for the judging of His people:

5“Gather to Me My holy ones,

those having cut a covenant with Me by sacrifice.”

6And let the heavens declare His righteousness—

for God Himself is Judge.a

Selah

7“Hear, O My people, and I will speak;

O Israel, and I will testify against you:

I am God, your God.

8It is not over your sacrifices that I rebuke you,

or your burnt offerings before me continually.

9I will not take a bull from your house

or male goats from your folds.

10For every living thing of the forest is unto Me,

and the cattle on a thousand hills.

11I know every bird of the mountains,

and the wild beast of the field is unto Me.

12If I were hungry, I would not say it to you,

for the world and its fullness is to Me.

13Do I eat the flesh of bulls,

and do I drink the blood of goats?

14Make a sacrifice of thanksgiving to God,

and fulfill to the Most High your vows.

15And call on Me in the day of distress,

and I will rescue you, and you will glorify Me.”

16And to the wicked God says,

“What to you is to recount My statutes,

and to lift up My covenant upon your mouth?

17And you have hated instruction,

and cast My words behind you!

18If you see a thief, then you are pleased with him,

and your portion is with those committing adultery.

19With your mouth you send forth evil,

and your tongue weaves deceit.

20You sit against your brother and speak;

against the son of your mother you place fault.

21These things you have done, and I kept silent;

you thought I was justb like you.

I will rebuke you

and set in array before your eyes.c

22Understand this, please, you forgetting God,

lest I tear you in pieces,

and there is no one to deliver!

23The one making a sacrifice of thanksgiving honors Me,

and he who sets his path,

I will make to look on the salvation of God.”

For the one directing.

Notes

  1. a 50.6 Or for He is a God of justice
  2. b 50.21 Or you thought the ‘I AM’ was
  3. c 50.21 Or and accuse you to your face
