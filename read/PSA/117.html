Psalm 117 DRAFT

Extol Him, All You Peoples

1Praise YHWH, all you nations;

adore Him, all you peoples!a

2For mighty is His loving devotion toward us,

and the faithfulness of YHWH is forever.

Hallelu YAH!b

Notes

  1. a 117.1 Cited in Romans 15:11
  2. b 117.2 Hallelu YAH means Praise YHWH
