Psalm 121

I lift Up My Eyes to the Hills

A Song for the Ascents.

1 I lift up my eyes to the hills,

from where my help does come.

2My help is from YHWH,

the Maker of the heavens and the earth.

3He will not give your foot to the slipping;

the One guarding you will not slumber.

4Behold, He does not slumber,

and He does not sleep—the One guarding Israel.

5YHWH is the One guarding you;

YHWH is your shade on your right hand.

6By day the sun will not strike you,

nor the moon in the night.

7YHWH will guard you from all evil;

He will guard your soul.

8YHWH will guard your going out and your coming in,

from now even to forever.
