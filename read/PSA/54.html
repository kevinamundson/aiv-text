Psalm 54

Save Me by Your Name

(1 Samuel 23:7–29)

A Maskil† of David, at the coming of the Ziphites, when they said to Saul, “Is not David hiding himself with us?”

1 O God, by Your name save me,

and by Your might defend me! a

2O God, hear my prayer,

give ear to the words of my mouth.

3For strangers have risen up against me,

and terrible ones have sought my soul;

they have not put God before them.

Selah

4behold, God is the One giving help to me;

the Lord is with those upholding my soul.

5He will return the evil to my enemies—

in Your faithfulness put an end to them!

6With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to You;

I will give thanks to Your name, O YHWH, for it is good.

7For from all distress He has delivered me,

and on my enemies my eye has looked.

For the one directing. With stringed instruments.

Notes

  1. a 54.1 Maskil is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
