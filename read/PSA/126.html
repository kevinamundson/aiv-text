Psalm 126 DRAFT

Zion’s Captives Restored

A Song of the Ascents.

1 In YHWH turning back the captivity of Zion,a

we were like those dreaming.b

2Then our mouth was filled with laughter,

and our tongue with a shout of joy.

Then they said among the nations,

“YHWH, has done great things for these.”

3YHWH has done great things for us;

and we came to be glad.

4Bring back, O YHWH, our captives,c

like streams in the Negev!

5Those sowing in tears

will reap with a shout of joy.

6Going forth, he who goes and weeps,

bearing the basket of seed,

coming in, he comes with a shout,

bearing his sheaves.

Notes

  1. a 126.1 Or bringing back the captives to Zion or restoring the fortunes of Zion
  2. b 126.1 Or like those restored to health
  3. c 126.4 Or Restore, O YHWH, our fortunes
