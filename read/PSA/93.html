Psalm 93 DRAFT

Yahweh Reigns!

(Psalm 99:1–9)

1YHWH reigns! In majesty He is robed.

YHWH has girded Himself with strength.

Surely the world is established;

no more can it be shaken.

2Your throne is established from that time;

You are King from everlasting.

3The floodwaters have lifted up, O YHWH,

the streams have lifted up their voice;

the streams have lifted up their breakers.

4More than the voices of many waters,

more majestic than the breakers of the sea,

majestic on high is YHWH!

5Your testimonies are fully confirmed;

Your house is lovely in holiness, O YHWH,

for the length of days.
