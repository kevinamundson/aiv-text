Psalm 133

How Pleasant to Live in Harmony!

(1 Corinthians 1:10–17; Ephesians 4:1–16)

A Song of the Ascents. Of David.

1 Behold, how good and how pleasant

is the dwelling of brothers even together!

2It is good like oil upon the head,

running down upon the beard,

the beard of Aaron,

which is running down upon the mouth of his garments;

3or like the dew of Hermon,

which is coming down upon the mountains of Zion.

For there YHWH commanded the blessing—

life even to forever!
