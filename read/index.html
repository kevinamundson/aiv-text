How to read this page

  • Italic words are supplied by the translator for sense in English.
  • Red words are the words of Jesus.
  • Headings, parallel references and the lettered notesa are editorial aids. They are not part of the Scripture text.

For developers and Scripture apps

  • USX 3.1 files, one per book: /usx/{BOOK}.usx (for example /usx/GEN.usx)
  • manifest.json: book list, counts and sha256 per USX file
  • books.json: the 66 books and their USX paths
  • metadata.json: translation metadata and licence

All of these are served with Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *. Styling follows the Paratext default USFM stylesheet (usfm.css).
